# Changelog

## Phases 6–8 · Bring Your Own Evals, MCP tooling and agents, scale and demo mode

### Phase 6 · Bring Your Own Evals (§6.1–6.9)
- **Evals** (Workbench → AI and platform) with Skills, Data, Runs and results, Model right-sizing, Quality gate and Regression watch, a skill picker, the eval-credit meter against a monthly budget, and the non-inferiority margin (default 2 quality points).
- **Skill registry** with four seeded skills (Find work email, Company research, Summarise website, Classify ICP fit), each described by a manifest: endpoint (MCP or HTTP), input and output schema, meter mapping, default and allowed models, timeout, side-effects flag and PII policy. The **Register a skill** wizard fetches a manifest URL or connects an MCP server (simulated), validates it, offers one-click fixes, stores the key in a masked vault (only a fingerprint is kept), runs a test call and shows a pass or fail checklist. The example manifest fails (side effects, no idempotency, 45 s timeout) until fixed.
- **Bring your data**: upload an anonymised CSV (parsed in the browser; names, emails and phones masked; only the header, count and three masked rows kept), sample tenant data with masking, or generate a synthetic set from a described distribution. Golden labels optional; region, retention and Delete dataset; a warning under 100 rows.
- **Synthetic test tenants**: every run provisions an isolated `sbx-…` tenant mirroring the pack's plans, meters and limits with 50 simulated users and 3 workflows. Runs cost eval credits and are refused over budget.
- **Suites**: accuracy, rubric judge (calibrated against 40 human labels, 91% agreement), schema compliance, latency, cost per run, robustness, refusal and contact-data accuracy, each with an interval and pass, warn or fail. Rubric editor, 1–5 repeat runs with the spread between repeats.
- **Model right-sizing**: per skill, quality with interval, cost per run, latency and credits per run; the cheapest model inside the margin is recommended in one sentence. The impact panel (cohort that burns credits fastest) shows credits saved per run, spend per workspace, margin per credit, run-out share, and net revenue and gross profit this month and over 12 months, driven by a scenario slider. **Create experiment** drafts a model-switch experiment with user-reported quality and rerun rate as guardrails.
- **Quality gate**: pass, warn, fail or no eval for every skill and model; experiments and default changes that touch a skill are blocked on fail unless an override with a reason is approved (audited). Canary evals run every simulated day on live model-switch experiments and trip the stop-loss.
- **Regression watch**: scheduled re-evals (on provider change, daily or weekly), 30-day drift charts with provider changes marked, alerts routed to Ask CB, and a drill to simulate a provider update.
- **Results**: leaderboard, cost-versus-quality frontier with the Pareto line, suites matrix, per-example side-by-side drill-down, failure clusters in plain language, and **Explain** in Ask CB.
- **Seeded scenario**: Company research scores 92 on the default model, 91 on the mid-tier model at 38% of the cost and 84 on the small model at 9%; the mid-tier model is recommended. Find work email's cheaper model fails the gate.

### Phase 7 · MCP tooling, agent workflows and the embed SDK (§7.1–7.7)
- **MCP Console**: eight servers (Knowledge Base, Data Lookup, Onboarding, plus Credits, Growth, Evals, Offers, Portal Experience) and 35 tools with tiers, the tool input editor (hide fields, defaults, min and max; `grant_reward` capped at 50 with a 7-day default expiry), try-it with dry run and idempotency keys, pending approvals, the activity log with **Explain this action**, MCP resources (ledger snapshots, policy documents, Memory cards) and prompts (every slash command), and the global kill switch.
- **One governed gateway** for people, Ask CB, agents and external clients: tenant isolation, scoped tokens per role, server scopes, account scopes, rate limits, idempotency, dry-run on every write, masked audit with prompt, arguments, outcome and approver. Four tiers: Read, Draft, Execute with approval, Auto within budget (credits per day, spend per day, concurrent experiments, approved by a person). A write never executes without an approver.
- **Connect a client** (simulated): a scoped token shown once, stored as a fingerprint; revoke.
- **Custom portal experiences**: plain words → a Moment definition (trigger, audience, content, action, frequency cap, experiment hook, locales, version) validated against a schema, JSON and plain-language summary side by side, live preview against real accounts and in the Credits Hub, publish behind approval, versions and one-click rollback. Published moments show in the Credits Hub when their trigger holds and respect the frequency cap.
- **Agents**: nine scripted agents (Onboarding, Usage Coach, Top-up Concierge, Dunning Rescue, Hypothesis, Experiment Operator, Eval, Win-back, Policy Sentinel) making real tool calls through the gateway, with trigger, autonomy, success metric, status, run timeline (ToolCallTrace), pending approvals, credits granted today and a switch per agent. Agents whose trigger fires run once a simulated day.
- **Embed SDK**: a `<cb-credits-hub>` web component snippet with modules, drawer or modal, Tenant Pack theme, SSO token and event hooks; a mock tenant app with **Embedded** and **Hosted** modes and an event log.
- **External agent demo**: a generic AI client on Dev's workspace calls `get_balance` then `estimate_run_cost`; `set_auto_topup` goes to approval; asking for another account is refused.

### Phase 8 · Scale, release cadence, hardening and demo mode (§8.1–8.8)
- **SeatCo**, a fourth archetype (seats plus a shared credit pool); every pack carries its archetype. **Pack Builder**: archetype, meters, plans and grants, rollover and expiry, top-ups, limits, rewards and tax display, with a live Credits Hub preview, **Pack lint** (expiry shorter than rollover, negative balance without a cap, missing tax display in a billed region, reward cap above forecast breakage) and load-as-tenant behind approval. - **Product health**: the north-star metric (monetization-moment conversion value per active account), seven inputs, three guardrails and the event taxonomy.
- **Trust and security**: tenant isolation tests and six prompt-injection tests that run the real code (help chat, pasted text, dataset rows, tool output, a Moment prompt, a CSV), PII masking, the consent ledger, retention and deletion for datasets and Memory cards with a deletion log, region labels, least-privilege roles and a masked audit export. Ask CB now treats pasted content as data.
- **Presenter mode** (header **Present**, Alt+Shift+P): persona, tenant and Horizon, 14 named moments with presenter notes (Alt+Shift+N / B), fast-forward (Alt+Shift+F), reset, a 12-minute timer, the pitch mapping, and a pseudo-locale switch (Alt+Shift+L).
- **Quality bar**: 95 unit tests; Phase 6–8 journeys (14 plus a 360px check) including an automated accessibility check on every new screen; Phase 2 and Phases 3–5 journeys re-run and passing; reduced motion honoured app-wide; the header fits from 360px to desktop. Performance measured on the production build (desktop 1.2 s warm to an interactive Credits Hub; 9.6 s at 4× CPU on 4G, over the 2.5 s budget: see known gaps).

## Phases 3–5 · Agentic onboarding, Ask CB, and the Growth Workbench loop

### Phase 3 · Agentic onboarding and the agentic tour (§3.1–3.7)
- **Get started** is Nia's Workbench entry. **Connect and consent** lists six sources (three Chargebee-native, three connectors) with why each is read, what is read and stored, retention, scopes and the data region; the minimum set is pre-selected; declining a source says what the agent won't know and never blocks onboarding.
- **Ingestion with real progress**: seven lanes (regional rules, customers, pricing, feature usage, models and providers, behaviour, help chat) with queued / connecting / reading / understanding / needs you / done states, live counts over the tenant's data and "what we learned" chips. Chargebee-native data starts at once; connectors connect first and offer **Use Chargebee data only for now**.
- **Memory cards** for every learned fact (source, confidence, last updated, Edit, Forget/Restore); up to five clarifying questions answered by chips, each skippable, logged and written back to the cards.
- **The reveal**: "Your monetization, in one view" with five findings (evidence, an impact range labelled as an estimate, Explore), a live **Time to first experiment** clock from Connect, and **Launch your first experiment**.
- **The agentic tour**: the agent drives the real Workbench in a sandbox copy (cohorts → at-risk free cohort → Ask CB → three ranked hypotheses → pick → draft with audience, variant, holdout, guardrails and sample size → D1/D3/D7 scorecard preview → approval → launch). Pause, Take over at any step, Skip to Ask CB, Replay, Exit, keyboard shortcuts (Alt+P/T/S/R/X), voice narration, reduced motion; progress persists per persona. The launch is the only live write and needs a tap.
- **Trust and failure states** as demo drills: a source fails (retry or continue without it), a slow connector, partial data, consent withdrawn mid-run (lanes stop, cards forgotten), a region with no policy pack (the agent asks), and resume after closing the tab.
- **Instrumentation**: every lane, question, card edit, reveal view and tour step is an event with a timestamp and simulated time; Get started shows Time to first experiment and drop-off by step.

### Phase 4 · Ask CB (§4.1–4.8)
- One engine, two scopes: **Ask CB** for Nia and Sam (drawer from every screen, a full page, and slash commands in ⌘K) and the **Credits assistant** for Maya, Dev and a Member, which only sees the user's own workspace.
- **Model switcher** per conversation from the Tenant Pack's assistant catalog (CB Lite, Swift, Deep, with speed, depth and relative cost); **Auto** routes lookups to the fast model and analysis to the strong one and says which it chose. "Used N CB credits" after each answer.
- **CB Daily 5**: 5 free CB credits a day per merchant user (answer 1, experiment draft 2, research 3), bonuses for launching with guardrails, recording a learning and launching the daily experiment, and a designed empty moment (wait until tomorrow, use CB Lite, add credits). Rules live in the Tenant Pack (`growth.assistant`).
- **Voice mode**: push-to-talk and hands-free with a live transcript, interrupt, a short spoken answer with details on screen; graceful fallback to typing; voice can never approve a write.
- **Research analyst**: a visible plan of sub-questions and a brief with citations into an 18-card Growth Knowledge Base (illustrative, each with source, as-of date and confidence); save to the Playbook.
- **Slash commands** with autocomplete, argument chips and a preview line: /credits, /forecast, /why, /cohort, /hypotheses, /experiments, /scorecard, /eval, /offer, /grant, /policy, /mcp, /undo, /help. Every command is instrumented against the click path (Ask CB → Records). /credits ships first: Crawl shows only /credits and /help.
- **Conversation design**: answer first, then evidence, confidence, the Memory cards used and next-action chips; **Show my work** reveals the tool calls; live components in answers (tables, bars, charts, hypothesis cards, scorecards, plan diffs); one clarifying question when money or customers are ambiguous; specific copy for empty results, tool errors, timeouts and rate limits (failure drills in Ask CB → Autonomy).
- **Autonomy per workflow** (grants, offers, experiments, dunning rescue): Suggest, Draft, Execute with approval, Auto within budget (grants up to 50 credits a day). Every write goes through the ApprovalSheet and the growth audit log with the originating conversation; /undo reverses the last change.
- **Scripted library**: 26 merchant prompts and 6 end-user prompts run end to end on the deterministic provider, computed from live data. An optional live-model fallback answers unscripted questions.
- **Red team**: ten attempts to read other users' data from an end-user thread all decline (Ask CB → Records, and the tests).

### Phase 5 · Growth Workbench (§5.1–5.10)
- **Today**: MRR movements (new, expansion, contraction, churn), credit burn, top-up attach rate, breakage share, margin per credit by model, the free-to-paid funnel and voluntary versus involuntary churn, each tile drilling into a cohort or the ledger.
- **Cohorts**: a retention heatmap by signup week, sliceable by region, plan, acquisition source, account age and behaviour; At risk and Thriving lanes with three plain-language drivers each; what thriving workspaces do differently with effect sizes; Save as audience, Revenue at stake, Nudge toward thriving behaviours.
- **Hypotheses**: three a day with a fixed anatomy, one recommended with a reason; pick, edit, approve and launch; a Learning library; launching the daily experiment earns CB credits.
- **Experiments**: builder with intervention type, audience, allocation, a 5–10% holdout, minimum detectable effect and sample-size calculator, mutual-exclusion layers, pre-registered metrics, guardrails with a stop-loss, and a sample-ratio check. States draft → approved → running → read-out → shipped or killed (plus paused and reverted).
- **Scorecards** at D1 (leading, never a winner), D3 (directional with intervals) and D7 (decision grade with intervals and segment cuts); guardrails always shown; verdict chips; Ship to 100% needs approval and keeps a revert.
- **Time machine** (+1, +3, +7 days) moves the whole simulated clock so the loop runs in minutes.
- **Experiment plays**: twelve Clay-relevant plays with simulator-based priors, and a dashboard of experiments in flight, win rate, cumulative revenue impact and learning velocity.
- **Churn arrest**: failed-payment funnel (soft versus hard), recovery by retry strategy, pre-dunning and card updates, revenue at risk, an arrest queue with recommended actions, levers (grace with credits working, grace window, retry timing, message variant, backup-method prompts) and a Dunning Rescue agent that drafts actions for approval.
- **Plan optimiser**: four behavioural personas (Explorer, Operator, Scaler, Agency), a replay of 90 days of usage against plan variants, run the winner as a sandbox and then as an experiment for new customers only, with an explicit grandfathering policy and its impact.
- **Breakage pool**: credits expiring unused by cohort, a recognised-breakage policy and a funding rule; funds upsell credits (7-day expiry) and use-or-extend nudges, refuses anything beyond the rule, never moves one customer's paid credits to another, with a finance note.
- **Rewards studio**: reward rules (qualifying action, amount, cap, expiry, eligibility, funding source), A/B tests that ship into the Tenant Pack and revert, low-value actions refused, and an abuse monitor.
- The Growth layer reads the 90-day population simulator, scaled from its 2,000-workspace sample to the tenant's base (`growth.sampleScale`, 25 for every pack). Churn arrest, Plan optimiser, Unit economics and MCP keep their billing-sample views below the new sections, labelled as such.
- Tests: `tests/phase345.test.ts` (22 tests covering every Phase 3–5 acceptance check) and `tests/journeys/phase345.journeys.mjs` (seven journeys plus the 360px check). Saved data gains a `growth` section without losing anything.
